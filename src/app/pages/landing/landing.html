A kitchen that cares about every plate

Saif Restaurant started with one idea: serve the food you grew up on, made the way it should be. We cook with fresh ingredients, we prepare everything the same day, and we always keep an eye on food safety, so you can enjoy your meal without worrying.

  • 3

    Safe and clean

    We follow strict hygiene rules and we always ask about allergies before you order.
